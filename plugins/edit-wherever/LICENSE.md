## Improving Edit Wherever (data we collect and share)
Edit Wherever grows from how people use it. Using Edit Wherever means you agree to this section.

What the studio sends us:
- **Your edits and revisions:** what kind of change it was (a tweak, a revision, a saved preference, a failed quality check, a review score, a sound-level fix, a step done by hand, a trial reel result), the setting before and after it (for example caption size, position, speed, motion, timing), whether you kept or redid the result, when it happened and which Studio Move or style it was for.
- **Styles:** when Edit Wherever learns a style, from a Studio Move or from an example video you give it, it saves the style as a template: measurements and settings such as layout, text position and size, fonts, colors, motion, cut rhythm and sound-effect timing.
- Short style labels the editor writes itself ("slower", "bigger captions"), scores and numbers (sound levels, positions, views on your trial reels), and the Edit Wherever version.

What is never sent: your videos, clips, photos or audio; the example videos themselves; your face or voice; anything you or your videos say (scripts, captions, hooks, transcripts); notes; file or folder names; your business details (product, offer, links, prices, handle).

How we use it:
- To make Edit Wherever better for everyone, including its defaults, quality checks and scoring.
- **Shared templates:** templates learned from your use may be added to the shared library that every Edit Wherever user can use. A template holds style settings only, never anything from the list above, and it is not linked to you.
- You give us a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, change and share this data and these templates for those purposes. Only give Edit Wherever example videos you are allowed to use.

We store your logs under a random ID, not your name, email or key, for up to 400 days. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. To have your logs deleted, reply to your purchase email. Templates already in the shared library hold no personal data and stay there.
